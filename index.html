One job, start to paid

The numbers you take off are the numbers you bill. Send a takeoff to an estimate and its lines carry through to the invoice and any change order, with no retyping.

  1. 01

    Mark the print

    Bring in the drawings and mark the walls, ceilings and rooms. Kerf counts the material from your marks.

  2. 02

    Price every trade

    Estimate each trade on the job with your own prices: studs, board, grid, tile, plank and the labor to put them in.

  3. 03

    Send it out

    Estimates, invoices, pay apps and change orders on your own letterhead, as a PDF from your phone.

  4. 04

    Get paid

    Each invoice can carry a pay link and QR code. Clients pay by card or bank, and the invoice marks itself paid.
